# Round 2 findings

## High
- The live admin PIN remains hard-coded in a public repository, the plan would publish it in the docs, and the in-memory three-attempt lockout resets on reload, so anyone can reach Confirm, Void, and Clear on the counter iPad. (Rotate the exposed PIN before trial, provision a non-default device-local admin credential instead of committing it, persist rate-limit state across reloads, and add a separate durable confirmation for destructive actions.)
- The TYPED desk gate is vacuous for an already-enrolled stolen number because the kiosk supplies the victim's saved name and the later POS name-to-number comparison necessarily matches, allowing an attacker to bill the victim without ever claiming or proving a name. (Make typed entry staff-authorized at the time of purchase after the desk looks the member up from independently stated identity, or capture an independently entered name without revealing the saved name and quarantine mismatches before an item can be selected.)
- Leaving the success screen early through Undo can leave its five-second timeout armed, so that stale callback can fire during the next member's session and force the kiosk to idle mid-purchase. (Keep a success-timer handle or generation token, cancel it on Undo and every early exit, make the callback verify the same sale and `success` state, and test Undo followed immediately by a new scan and purchase.)
- `Clear all data` still permits deletion while unbatched sales exist and destroys enrollments plus the entire processed audit history, so one mistake or misuse of the exposed admin credential can erase the very sales the loss-window work is meant to protect. (Refuse clearing whenever any non-voided sale is waiting, require a verified export before deleting sales, separate bounded history pruning from enrollment reset, and remove or additionally gate full wipe in the production build.)
- Every mutation is an unlocked whole-record load/modify/save, so a second Safari/home-screen context or stale pre-v2 page can silently overwrite a newer sale or batch state and can render or confirm a voided sale as chargeable. (Define a single-writer mechanism with persisted revision checks and fail-closed conflict handling, detect and block stale builds/secondary contexts, and test interleavings among sale, batch, void, and confirm writes including an old client.)

## Medium
- A crash after `addSale` commits but before the success screen is visible leaves no recovery cue, so the member's natural retry records a duplicate charge with a new UUID. (Persist and surface the last committed sale on restart for a short correction window, or carry a stable purchase-attempt id across retry, and test a crash immediately after successful storage followed by the same tap.)
- A void records only `voidedAt`, leaving no reason or action source and no way to distinguish a legitimate correction from unauthorized removal when reconciling a changed Pending report. (Persist an immutable void record containing sale ID, timestamp, source surface, and required reason, render it on the report, and test that retries cannot rewrite the original audit fields.)
