Setting Up a New Counter iPad

Start to finish, about 30 minutes. Do it at the counter, with the stand and the charging cable where they'll live.

What you need

  • The iPad, charged or plugged in
  • The long charging cable and the stand
  • The gym Wi-Fi password
  • The admin PIN for the kiosk (from the manager — it's never written down here)
  • Your own gym keytag, and your phone with your gym barcode on it
  • A 4–6 digit code to use for Guided Access (you'll make this up in Step 9 — give it to the manager)

Replacing an old iPad? Read this first

Sales don't move between iPads. Each iPad keeps its own list. A new iPad starts completely empty.

If the old iPad still turns on, before you do anything else:

  1. On the old iPad, open admin (press and hold "Scan your gym barcode" for 1 second → PIN).
  2. Tap Create batch → Print → type the sales into the gym software → Confirm keyed. (Same as a normal pull — see the Desk Manual, section 7.)
  3. Check any other batch marked Pending — print and key those too.
  4. Only now put the old iPad away.

Old iPad dead and won't turn on? Any sales that weren't printed yet are gone. Tell the manager so they know.

Members will be asked their name once more the first time they scan on the new iPad. That's normal — warn the staff.

Step 1 — Basic iPad setup

Brand-new or just reset? Follow Apple's on-screen setup: pick English, connect to the gym Wi-Fi, and sign in with the gym's Apple account if it has one (skip it if not). Turn on nothing extra.

Already set up? Just make sure it's on the gym Wi-Fi: Settings → Wi-Fi.

Step 2 — Update the iPad

Settings → General → Software Update. If there's an update, install it now, before setup — not in the middle of a busy day.

Step 3 — Open the kiosk in Safari

Open Safari (the blue compass — not Chrome, the next step only works in Safari). Type this address exactly:

wyattfulkerson11-boop.github.io/iron-jungle-sales/

You should see the Iron Jungle logo and "Scan your gym barcode."

Step 4 — Add it to the Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up) at the top.
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Name it Iron Jungle → Add.

Step 5 — Close Safari for good

Swipe up from the bottom and hold to see open apps, then swipe Safari away.

From now on, only ever open the kiosk from the Iron Jungle icon. Safari and the icon keep separate lists — sales tapped in Safari won't show up in the icon. And if both are open at once you'll get "Another copy of the kiosk is open."

Step 6 — Open it from the icon and allow the camera

Tap the Iron Jungle icon. When it asks to use the camera, tap Allow.

Tapped "Don't Allow" by mistake? Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow, then close and reopen the icon.

Leave it open for a minute on Wi-Fi. That lets it save itself so it still opens if the Wi-Fi drops later.

Step 7 — Never let the screen turn off

Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never

Plug it in with the long cable and leave it plugged in, always.

Step 8 — Put it on the stand

Set it where it'll actually live, facing where members stand at the fridge. Make sure the camera (top edge) isn't blocked by the stand and isn't pointing straight into a ceiling light.

Step 9 — Turn on Guided Access

Guided Access locks the iPad to the kiosk so members can't leave it or open other apps.

  1. Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn it On.
  2. Tap Passcode Settings → Set Guided Access Passcode. Pick a code and give it to the manager.
  3. Open the Iron Jungle icon.
  4. Triple-click the side (top) button → tap Start in the top right.

To get out later: triple-click the side button again → enter the code → End.

Step 10 — Check the storage (1 minute)

  1. Press and hold "Scan your gym barcode" for about 1 second → type the admin PIN.
  2. At the bottom, tap Check limit → OK. Wait a few seconds.
  3. It says how many MB and roughly how many sales fit. Write it here: ________ MB

While you're there, note the build number at the bottom: ________________ (useful if something's wrong later). Tap Back to kiosk.

Step 12 — Wipe your test sales

Don't type the test sales into the gym software. They're fake.
  1. In admin, on the test batch you just made, tap Confirm keyed. (This is the one time you confirm without keying — they're only tests.)
  2. Tap Clear all data → type CLEAR in capitals → OK.
  3. It should say "All data cleared." and "Nothing waiting."

If it says it can't clear because sales are waiting or a batch is pending, you missed step 1 — make a batch of what's left and confirm it, then try again.

Tap Back to kiosk. Make sure Guided Access is on (triple-click → Start).

Step 13 — Ready for members

  • The counter sign is standing next to the iPad
  • The paper sheet, the exception log and the clipboard are behind the counter
  • The Desk Manual is in the binder at the counter
  • The manager has the admin PIN and the Guided Access code
  • Staff know members will be asked their name once more
  • iPad is plugged in, on the stand, in Guided Access, showing "Scan your gym barcode"
