1. What the iPad does

It replaces the paper sheet where members write down drinks and snacks they take.

  • The member scans their gym barcode (keytag or phone) and taps what they took.
  • The iPad keeps a list. Nobody pays on the iPad.
  • Twice a day, someone at the desk prints the list and types it into the gym software — just like the paper sheet.
Sales are saved on the iPad itself. Closing the app or restarting the iPad does not lose them. They're only "safe for good" once they're typed into the gym software.

2. Your job at the counter

When someone walks up with a drink or snack, say this — that's the whole script:

"Scan your barcode on the iPad there — it's the same as writing your name."
  • Don't explain how it works. Don't apologise for it. If they're confused, point at the sign.
  • If it isn't working for them, hand them the paper sheet (section 5). Don't argue, don't troubleshoot while they wait.
  • The paper sheet lives behind the counter, not on it. Only bring it out when the iPad can't take the sale.

4. Helping a member who's stuck

"The camera won't read my card."

  1. Hold it flat and level, in the middle, so the barcode fills the frame side to side. (The corner brackets are just decoration.)
  2. Phone barcode? Turn the phone's brightness up and tilt it away from the ceiling lights.
  3. Still no? Tap No card? Type your member number (see next).
  4. Still no? Hand over the paper sheet.

"I don't have my card."

  1. On the scan screen, tap No card? Type your member number.
  2. They type the number that's printed under the barcode on their keytag, plus their name, then Continue.
  3. If it says "That number and name don't match", the number belongs to someone else. Don't try to get around it — hand over the paper sheet.

Sales typed this way print as TYPED so the desk can double-check them later (section 8).

"I tapped the wrong thing."

On the ✓ screen, tap Undo — I didn't mean that (they have 5 seconds). The sale is cancelled. Then scan again and pick the right one.

Missed the 5 seconds? Tell the desk — someone with the PIN cancels it (section 9).

"It says the wrong name." / "That's not me."

Scan the card, then tap Not you? next to the name. It asks "Whose card is this?" — type the right name and save. Old sales keep the old name; new ones use the new name.

If they tapped it by accident, No, I'm [name] goes back with nothing changed.

"I scanned, but I changed my mind."

Tap Cancel at the bottom. Nothing is charged.

5. When to hand over the paper sheet

Hand it over straight away, no argument, any time the iPad can't take the sale:

  • The camera won't start, or won't read the card, and typing the number doesn't work
  • The screen is frozen, or says "Another copy of the kiosk is open"
  • The iPad is dead, updating, or missing
  • The member doesn't want to use it, is in a hurry, or is annoyed. Don't try to talk them into it.
Every time the paper comes out, write one line on the log (it's on the clipboard with the paper). It takes 5 seconds. Plain words are fine: "camera wouldn't read", "guy didn't want to", "iPad was dead".

6. Opening the admin screen

  1. On the scan screen, press and hold the words "Scan your gym barcode" for about 1 second.
  2. Type the admin PIN (ask your manager — it's never written down here).
  3. You're on Sales admin. Tap Back to kiosk when you're done.
  • Wrong PIN 3 times = locked out for 30 seconds. Just wait.
  • Admin closes itself after 3 minutes of nobody touching it. That's normal — reopen it.
  • Never leave admin open and walk away.

7. Pulling the sales (twice a day)

When: once at opening and once at midday. Also any time the admin screen shows "oldest waiting … — key it in" in red (that means a sale has been sitting over 6 hours).

  1. Open the admin screen (section 6).
  2. Tap Create batch. This bundles up every sale waiting to be typed in. (Greyed out and says "Nothing waiting"? There's nothing to do.)
  3. On the new batch, tap View and look it over.
  4. Tap Print. No printer? On the print screen, use its share button to save/send it as a PDF.
    Always use Print, not the Share button — Share sends a messy text file.
  5. Type every line into the gym software, like you do with the paper sheet. Check TYPED and VOID lines as you go (section 8).
  6. Only after every line is in the gym software, tap Confirm keyed. The batch changes from Pending to Keyed in.
Never tap Confirm keyed before the sales are actually typed in. Until you confirm, the batch stays Pending and you can reprint it exactly the same if the paper gets lost. Confirm too early and those sales could be missed.

Keep the printed sheet. The iPad forgets batches after 30 days — the paper is the permanent record. File it wherever your manager says.

Got interrupted halfway? Nothing's lost. The batch stays Pending. Come back, Print it again, finish, then confirm.

8. Checking TYPED and VOID lines

TYPED lines

These are members who typed their number instead of scanning. They print with TYPED and an empty ☐ desk checked box.

  1. Look up the member number in the gym software.
  2. Does the name match? Type the sale in, and tick the box by hand.
  3. Doesn't match, or the number doesn't exist? Don't type it in. Write "no match" next to it and tell your manager.
The iPad can't tell if a number is real. A mistyped number looks totally normal on the sheet. Your check is the only thing that catches it — so actually check, every time.

VOID lines

These are crossed out and marked VOID. They were cancelled and are already left out of the total. Don't type them in.

If a line got voided after you already typed it in, fix it in the gym software.

9. Cancelling a sale that shouldn't count

For when a member missed the 5-second Undo, or something got charged by mistake:

  1. Open admin (section 6).
  2. Find the sale under Waiting to be batched (or View the Pending batch it's in).
  3. Tap Void on that line, then OK. This can't be undone — check it's the right line first.

Batch already Keyed in? The iPad won't let you void it. Fix it in the gym software instead.

11. Never do these

  • Never tap "Clear all data." That's for managers only. It deletes every member's name.
  • Never tap "Confirm keyed" before the sales are typed into the gym software.
  • Never open the kiosk in Safari or type in a different web address. Only use the Iron Jungle icon on the home screen.
  • Never unplug the iPad or turn off Guided Access and leave it that way.
  • Never give out the PIN or leave admin open.
  • Never argue with a member about using it. Hand them the paper.
